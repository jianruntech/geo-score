What bulb suits brass

Written by Editor, 1 September 2026

Brass looks best under warm light, between 2,200 and 2,700 kelvin, which brings out the yellow in the metal instead of turning it grey. A clear filament bulb shows off an open fitting; a frosted bulb suits a fitting where the bulb is on show.

What to look for

  • Warm colour temperature.
  • A dimmable fitting.
  • Shades that hide the bulb.

How the numbers compare
