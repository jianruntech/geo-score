How to light a hallway

Written by Editor, 1 September 2026

A hallway needs light at two heights: a pendant or ceiling light to fill the space, and wall lights at eye level so faces are not lit from above. In a narrow Victorian hall, wall lights alone are often enough and make the space feel wider.

What to look for

  • Warm colour temperature.
  • A dimmable fitting.
  • Shades that hide the bulb.

How the numbers compare
