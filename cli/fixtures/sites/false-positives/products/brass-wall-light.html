How the brass wall light is made

The brass wall light is spun from a single sheet, lacquered or left to age, and wired with braided flex in the colour you choose. It takes a standard bulb and works with most dimmers, so it can replace an existing fitting without new wiring.

What finishes are available

  • Polished and lacquered.
  • Unlacquered, to age naturally.
  • Antique bronze.

What it costs

From £185, with 1,400 sold since 2019 and 98% still in the original finish. Browse our lighting resources for fitting guides, and our installer resources for wiring diagrams.
