Lanternfield Journal Wall lights Pendants

How the linen pendant is made

The linen pendant is a hand-stitched drum shade on a brass gallery, hung from braided flex at the drop you specify. It softens light more than a glass shade and suits a dining table or a stairwell where glare would be a problem.

What sizes are available

  • Small, for a hall.
  • Medium, for a table.
  • Large, for a stairwell.

What it costs

From £240, in 3 sizes and 12 linens, with 640 made last year. Browse our lighting resources for fitting guides, and our installer resources for wiring diagrams.
