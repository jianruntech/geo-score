Lanternfield Journal Wall lights Pendants

Lighting made for old houses

Lanternfield makes wall lights and pendants for period homes, in brass, linen and glass, and wires every fitting by hand in our workshop. Each light is made to order, so you choose the finish, the flex colour and the length before it is built.

What we make

  • Brass wall lights.
  • Linen pendants.
  • Glass shades.

How long it takes

We shipped 1,850 lights last year; 92% left the workshop within 14 days and cost £85 to £420. Browse our lighting resources for fitting guides, and our installer resources for wiring diagrams.
