How to share results with your team

Most teams share Tidemark results in the pull request that introduced the problem. The CI integration posts one comment per run, updates it on the next push, and resolves it automatically once every new finding has been fixed.

Where results can go

  • A pull request comment.
  • A CI test report.
  • A chat channel through a webhook.

What to leave out

Links behind a login or on hosts that block automated requests only add noise. List them in the skip section of the configuration so the shared results stay short.
