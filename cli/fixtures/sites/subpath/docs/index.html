Check every link before you publish

Tidemark is a command-line tool that crawls a static site before you deploy it and reports every broken link, missing anchor and redirect chain. It runs in a few seconds on a laptop and fails your build when a page would ship with a dead link.

What Tidemark checks

  • Internal links and anchors.
  • External links, with a polite rate limit.
  • Redirect chains longer than two hops.
