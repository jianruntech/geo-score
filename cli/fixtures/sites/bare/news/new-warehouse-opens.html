New Warehouse Opens

The second warehouse on Mill Lane is now open and doubles the stock held for next day collection. All goods are sold in trade packs of 100, 250 and 1,000 pieces, and mixed packs can be made up at the counter for regular account holders who order more than once a month, with carriage charged at cost on orders below the free delivery value and waived on everything above it, while goods returned unopened within the month are credited in full less the carriage already paid, and goods returned opened are credited at the discretion of the counter manager, who also handles bulk quotations, special finishes, non-standard lengths and any order that needs to be cut or threaded to a drawing supplied by the customer, with lead times agreed at the point of order and confirmed in writing once the material has been allocated from stock.

Trade prices on application.
