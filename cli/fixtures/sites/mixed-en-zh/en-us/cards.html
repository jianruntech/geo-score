Tidewire Payments Transfers Cards

How company cards work

Company cards draw on the same multi-currency balance, so a purchase in euros is paid from euros without a conversion. Each card can carry its own limit and its own list of allowed merchants.

Who can hold a card?

Any team member you invite can hold a virtual card straight away, and a physical card arrives by post within a few working days.
