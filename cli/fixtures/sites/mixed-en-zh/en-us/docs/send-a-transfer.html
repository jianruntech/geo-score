Tidewire Payments Transfers Cards

Send a transfer

To send a transfer, add the supplier as a beneficiary, choose the currency and the amount, and confirm. The expected arrival date is shown before you confirm, and you can follow the transfer until it lands.

How to schedule a transfer

A transfer can also be scheduled for a later date or repeated every month, which suits rent, salaries and regular supplier invoices.
