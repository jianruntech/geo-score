Tidewire Payments Transfers Cards

How Tidewire payments work

Tidewire lets a business accept cards, bank transfers and wallets from customers abroad, and settles the money into a multi-currency balance. You choose which currencies to hold and when to convert them.

Which payment methods are supported?

Cards from the major networks, local bank transfers in the main markets and the most used digital wallets are all supported from a single integration.
