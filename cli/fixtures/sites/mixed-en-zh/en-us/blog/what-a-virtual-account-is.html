What is a virtual account?

A virtual account is a set of local bank details in another country that routes money into your balance. A customer in that country pays it like any domestic account, with no international wire.

When a virtual account helps

It helps whenever customers or marketplaces pay out in their own currency, because they can pay locally and you receive the money without a detour.
