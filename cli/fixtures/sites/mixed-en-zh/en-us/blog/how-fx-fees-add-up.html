How FX fees add up

A foreign-exchange fee is rarely one line on a statement. It is usually the gap between the rate a bank applies and the mid-market rate, and on a large invoice that gap costs more than any fixed fee.

Why the spread matters

The spread is charged on every conversion, so a business that converts each payment as it arrives pays it many times over in a single month.
