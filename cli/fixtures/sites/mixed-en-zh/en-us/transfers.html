How transfers reach suppliers

A transfer leaves your balance in the currency the supplier wants and arrives through a local payment network where one exists, which is usually faster and cheaper than an international wire.

What does a transfer cost?

A transfer in a local currency costs a flat fee, and a conversion adds a small margin above the mid-market rate, shown before you confirm.
