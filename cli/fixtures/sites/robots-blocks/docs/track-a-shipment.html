Track a shipment

Every booking gets a tracking page that updates at collection, at each hub scan and at delivery. You can share the page with your customer, and it shows the signature and a photo of the delivered load once the driver closes the job.

How tracking updates arrive

  • Collection scan from the driver's handset.
  • Hub scans at each depot the load passes.
  • Delivery signature and photo.

Where to find the tracking page

Open the booking from your dashboard and choose Share tracking. The link works without an account and expires thirty days after delivery.
