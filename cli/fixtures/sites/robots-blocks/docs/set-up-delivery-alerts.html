Set up delivery alerts

Delivery alerts tell you, and optionally your customer, when a load is collected, delayed or delivered. Alerts go by email or text message, and you choose which events trigger them for each booking or as a default for the whole account.

Which events can trigger an alert

  • Collection confirmed.
  • Delivery delayed beyond the booked slot.
  • Delivery completed with signature.

How to turn alerts on

Go to Settings, then Notifications, and tick the events you want. Changes apply to new bookings straight away and to existing bookings after their next scan.
