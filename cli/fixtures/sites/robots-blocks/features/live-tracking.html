How live tracking works

Live tracking combines the driver's handset scans with the vehicle's position, so you see where a load is between depots as well as when it was last scanned. The estimated arrival time updates as the vehicle moves, not only at each scan.

What you can see

  • The vehicle's position on a map.
  • An arrival estimate that updates as it moves.
  • Every scan with its time and depot.

Who can see it

Anyone with the tracking link can see the map and the arrival estimate. Only people on your account can see the booking details and the invoice.
