Import invoices from a spreadsheet

If you kept invoices in a spreadsheet before Fernhollow, you can bring them across in one upload. Each row becomes an invoice with its customer, date, amount and status, and paid invoices are matched to bank lines automatically.

What the template expects

  • One invoice per row, with a unique invoice number.
  • Dates written as day, month and year.
  • Amounts including VAT, without currency symbols.

How to upload the file

  1. Download the template.
  2. Paste your invoices into it.
  3. Upload the file under Invoices, Import.

Fernhollow checks every row before importing anything, and lists the rows it could not read so you can correct them and upload again.
