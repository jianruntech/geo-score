How to close the books in a day

Written by Mara Quint, updated 2 September 2026

Closing the books means confirming that every payment in and out of the business account has been matched to an invoice, a bill or a known expense, and that nothing is left unexplained. A one-day close is realistic for a workshop with fewer than 500 transactions a month.

What you need before you start

  • A bank feed that is up to date.
  • Every sales invoice issued in the month.
  • Receipts for card spending.

Which transactions take the longest

According to the ICAEW small business survey, 61% of reconciliation time goes on card payments without receipts, and 24% on customer payments that do not quote an invoice number. Fix those two and the rest is quick.

How to handle what is left

Anything still unmatched at the end of the day goes into a suspense list with a note. It is better to close on time with three open items than to hold the whole month open for them.
