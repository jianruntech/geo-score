How to choose sandpaper grit

Written by Dana Whitlow, 11 June 2026

Start with the coarsest grit that removes the defect you can see, then move up in steps of no more than half again. Skipping grits leaves scratches from the coarser paper that only show once the finish goes on.

Which grits to keep in stock

  • 80 for shaping and heavy stock removal.
  • 120 and 180 for general preparation.
  • 240 before an oil or wax finish.

How long a sheet lasts

According to independent abrasive tests published in 2025, ceramic sheets outlast aluminium oxide by 40% on hardwoods, though they cost 25% more per sheet.
