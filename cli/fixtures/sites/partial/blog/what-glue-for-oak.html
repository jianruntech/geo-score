What glue to use on oak

Written by Dana Whitlow, 2 May 2026

Oak is oily and acidic, so ordinary PVA glue can fail on joints that see moisture. A type two cross-linking PVA handles indoor furniture well, and a two-part epoxy is the safer choice for anything that lives outside.

What we sell most of

  • Cross-linking PVA for indoor joints.
  • Slow-cure epoxy for gates and benches.
  • Polyurethane glue for gap filling.

How often joints fail

Our returns data shows 12% of outdoor oak projects glued with plain PVA come back within a year, against 2.5% with epoxy, across 4,000 orders in 2025.
