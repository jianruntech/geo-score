Workshop supplies, delivered next day

Ridgeline Supply stocks abrasives, adhesives, fixings and hand tools for small joinery and furniture workshops. Orders placed before four in the afternoon leave the same day, and trade customers can pay on thirty-day terms once their account is open.

What we stock

  • Abrasives in every common grit.
  • Wood glues and epoxies.
  • Fixings, hinges and fittings.
  • Sharpening stones and honing guides.

How delivery works

We ship 3,200 orders a month from one warehouse, and 97% arrive the next working day. Orders over £60 ship free to any mainland address.
