How spot rates move

Spot rates follow the balance of loads and trucks on a lane, and they move faster than contract rates because nobody has agreed a price in advance.

On 312 lanes the spot rate rose 11% in the week before a holiday, and 64% of those lanes fell back within a fortnight.
