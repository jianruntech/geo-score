What soil carbon tells you

Soil organic carbon fell 12% on arable fields over two decades1, and grassland held 2.4 times as much per hectare[2].

Why it matters

Carbon holds water and structure in a soil, so a field that loses it dries out sooner and works harder.

  2. Grassland carbon study, carbon per hectare.
