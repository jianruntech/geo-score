Why fertiliser prices swing

Nitrogen fertiliser is made from natural gas, so its price follows gas prices with a lag of a few months.

Ammonium nitrate cost £285 a tonne in March and £410 in October, a rise of 44% in seven months.

Prices from the farm inputs price index.

Gas made up 80% of the cost of making it, as the fertiliser makers' association puts it.
