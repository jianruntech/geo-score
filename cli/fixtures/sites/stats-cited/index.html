Farm data you can check

Orchardline collects field and market data for mixed farms in England and publishes it with the source of every number beside it.

What the data shows

Wheat averaged 8.1 tonnes a hectare on 1,450 farms last harvest, and 23% of them used a cover crop, according to the national cereals survey.
