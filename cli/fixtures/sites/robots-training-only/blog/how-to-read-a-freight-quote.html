How to read a freight quote

Written by Priya Anand

A freight quote is made of three parts: the base rate for the lane, a fuel surcharge that moves with diesel prices, and any accessorial charges for tail lifts, timed deliveries or residential addresses. Only the base rate is fixed by the haulier alone.

What the surcharges cover

  • Fuel, reviewed monthly.
  • Tail-lift collection or delivery.
  • Timed or weekend delivery slots.

How big the fuel share is

According to DfT road freight statistics, fuel made up 31% of haulage operating costs in 2025, up from 24% in 2020, which is why surcharges are reviewed every month.
