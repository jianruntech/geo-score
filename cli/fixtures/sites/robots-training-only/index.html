Book freight without the phone calls

Harrowgate Freight books pallet and parcel freight for small shippers across the UK. You enter the collection and delivery postcodes, compare fixed quotes from vetted hauliers, and track the load from collection to signature without a single phone call.

What Harrowgate Freight does

  • Compares fixed quotes from vetted hauliers.
  • Books collection slots that suit your loading bay.
  • Tracks every load and alerts you on delay.

How quotes are calculated

Quotes cover 1,400 routes, 96% of them with same-day collection, and start from £45 per pallet. Each quote is fixed once booked, so the price on the invoice is the price you saw.
