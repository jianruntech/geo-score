Connect your bank account

Connecting a bank account gives Fernhollow a read-only feed of your transactions, refreshed every morning. Fernhollow can never move money, and you can revoke access from your banking app at any time without contacting us.

Before you connect

  • Make sure you can sign in to your online banking.
  • Have your banking app to hand for approval.
  • Decide which accounts belong to the business.

How the connection works

  1. Open Settings and choose Bank feeds.
  2. Pick your bank from the list.
  3. Approve access in your banking app.

The first import brings in the last ninety days of transactions. After that, new transactions arrive once a day, usually before eight in the morning.
