How Fernhollow pricing works

Fernhollow charges one flat monthly fee per business, with no limit on transactions, invoices or users. Every plan includes bank feeds and VAT returns; the larger plans add payroll and multi-currency accounts for workshops that sell abroad.

Which plan fits your workshop

PlanPriceBest for
Bench£12 a monthSole makers
Studio£29 a monthSmall teams
Works£59 a monthWorkshops with staff

What is not included

Prices exclude VAT, which is charged at 20% according to HMRC's published VAT rates. There is no setup fee and you can cancel at the end of any month.
