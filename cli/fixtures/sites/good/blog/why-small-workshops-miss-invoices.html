Why small workshops miss invoices

Written by Mara Quint, updated 30 July 2026

Most missed invoices are not forgotten jobs; they are finished jobs that were never written up because the maker moved straight on to the next commission. The fix is to raise the invoice at the moment the work is handed over, not at the end of the week.

Where the money leaks

  • Small repair jobs paid in cash and never recorded.
  • Deposits taken without a matching final invoice.
  • Materials recharged to a customer by message instead of invoice.

How big the gap is

A study of small business cash flow by Xero found that 1 in 5 invoices from sole traders is raised more than 30 days after the work, and that businesses invoicing within 48 hours are paid 11 days sooner on average.

What to do this week

Pick the three jobs you finished most recently and check that each has an invoice. If one does not, raise it today and set a reminder for the next handover.
