What changed in the VAT rules for 2026

Written by Mara Quint, updated 20 August 2026

Three things changed for small VAT-registered businesses in April 2026: the registration threshold moved, digital records became mandatory for every return, and late submissions now earn points before they earn fines. None of them needs new software, but all of them need a habit.

What the new threshold means

  • Registration is required above the new turnover threshold.
  • Voluntary registration is still allowed below it.
  • Deregistration rules did not change.

How the penalty points work

According to HMRC guidance on VAT penalties, a quarterly filer reaches the penalty threshold at 4 points, and each point expires after 24 months of on-time returns. The threshold rose to £90,000 in 2024 and has not moved since.

Which records to keep

Keep every sales and purchase invoice in digital form, together with the bank line that settled it. Fernhollow stores both side by side, so the record is complete the moment a payment is matched.
