How reconciliation works in Fernhollow

Reconciliation is the daily job of matching each bank transaction to the invoice, bill or expense it belongs to. Fernhollow proposes a match for every line it can explain, and asks you only about the lines it cannot, with the likeliest candidates listed first.

What gets matched automatically

  • Customer payments that quote an invoice number.
  • Card payments with a receipt photographed in the app.
  • Regular bills such as rent, insurance and software.

When you are asked to decide

A transaction is held for review when two invoices share the same amount, when a payment covers several invoices, or when the payee is new. Reviewing a held line takes one tap once you have checked it.
