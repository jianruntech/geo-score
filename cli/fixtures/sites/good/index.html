Bookkeeping that closes itself

Fernhollow is bookkeeping software for small workshops, studios and makers in the UK. It pulls in your bank feed every morning, matches each payment to the invoice it settles, and flags the few transactions that need a human decision, so the month-end close takes an afternoon instead of a week.

What Fernhollow does

  • Reconciles bank feeds from thirty UK banks every morning.
  • Chases unpaid invoices with polite, scheduled reminders.
  • Prepares VAT returns in the format HMRC expects.
  • Exports a clean ledger for your accountant.

How much time it saves

In our 2025 survey of 1,240 workshop owners, 38% said the monthly close took them more than three days. The Federation of Small Businesses reports that late payment costs a small firm an average of £22,000 a year, which is why reminders are built in.
