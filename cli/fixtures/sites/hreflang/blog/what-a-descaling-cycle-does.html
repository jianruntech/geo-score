What a descaling cycle does

By Mara Lindqvist, product engineer. Updated 8 September 2026.

A descaling cycle heats a mild acid solution to sixty degrees and holds it there for twenty minutes, long enough to dissolve the scale on the element without boiling the acid. The kettle then rinses itself twice with clean water. Citric acid works as well as the branded sachets, at a fraction of the price.

How often should the cycle run?

When the ring on the handle lights. In a hard-water area that is about once a month, and in a soft-water area two or three times a year.

Is the acid safe for the kettle?

At sixty degrees a citric acid solution dissolves limescale but does not attack stainless steel or the sealed element. Vinegar works too, though its smell lingers through a rinse or two. Strong descalers sold for coffee machines are not needed for a kettle and can mark the steel if they are left in too long.
