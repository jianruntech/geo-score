About Lumen Kettle

Lumen Kettle was started by two appliance engineers who were tired of replacing kettles that had failed from scale nobody could see. The company designs its kettles and their sensor in its own workshop and sells them in three countries.

Where are the kettles made?

The bodies are pressed and finished by a stainless steel workshop near the company's office, and the sensor boards are assembled by a small electronics firm in the same region. Every kettle is boiled fifty times before it leaves, and its reference boil time is reset so that the sensor learns the water of the home it goes to rather than the water of the workshop.

Spare bases, lids and filters are kept for ten years after a model is last sold, and the company repairs kettles sent back to it for the cost of the parts.
