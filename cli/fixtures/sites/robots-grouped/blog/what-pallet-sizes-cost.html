What pallet sizes cost to move

Written by Priya Anand

Pallet freight is priced by footprint and weight band, not by what is on the pallet. A full pallet takes a whole space on the trailer, so a half-height pallet only saves money when the haulier can stack another load on top of it.

Which size to choose

  • Quarter pallets for small, heavy loads.
  • Half pallets for stackable goods.
  • Full pallets for anything bulky.

How prices compare

The Palletways price index reports a national average of £62 for a full pallet, £48 for a half and £39 for a quarter pallet on a next-day service.
