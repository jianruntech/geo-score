Why freight quotes change

Written by Priya Anand

A quote changes between two searches because hauliers price the lane in real time: a trailer that is half full on Tuesday is cheaper to fill than an empty one on Friday. Booking early in the week usually finds the lower price for the same lane.

What moves the price

  • Spare capacity on the lane that day.
  • Fuel surcharge changes at month end.
  • Seasonal peaks before holidays.

When to book

Across 3,500 bookings last quarter, Tuesday quotes were 12% below Friday quotes and 18% below the pre-Christmas peak for the same lanes.
