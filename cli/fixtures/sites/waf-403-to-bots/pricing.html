How pricing works

Every quote on Pinecrest Freight is a fixed price for the lane, the pallet size and the service you choose. There is no subscription for occasional shippers; regular shippers can pay one flat monthly fee of £30 to unlock account rates on every lane.

What the fixed price includes

  • Collection and delivery on the booked days.
  • Tracking and delivery photo.
  • Basic cover for loss or damage.

What costs extra

Tail-lift vehicles, timed delivery slots and residential addresses are quoted as options before you book, so nothing is added afterwards.
