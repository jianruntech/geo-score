Setting up rooms and alerts

Every sensor belongs to a room, and every room has a lower limit, an upper limit and a delay. A reading outside the limits for longer than the delay opens an alert, which goes to the people on the rota for that room until someone records what was done about it.

What limits should a room have?

Use the limits your food safety or pharmacy plan already names. A chiller for food is commonly held between one and five degrees, a freezer below minus eighteen, and a pharmacy fridge between two and eight. Frostline does not suggest limits of its own: the plan your inspector reads is the one the alerts should follow.

Who gets the alert?

Add each person to the rota with a phone number and the hours they cover. Outside those hours the alert moves to the next person, and if nobody answers within the escalation time it goes to the site manager. Return to getting started to add more sensors.
