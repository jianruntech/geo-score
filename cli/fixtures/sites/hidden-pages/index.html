Frostline

Temperature monitoring for cold rooms

Frostline puts a wireless sensor in each cold room, freezer and refrigerated van, reads the temperature every minute, and sends an alert to the person on duty when a reading leaves the range you set. A kitchen, a pharmacy or a warehouse sees every room on one screen, and an inspector sees the whole record for any day.

How does the monitoring work?

Each sensor sends its reading over a low-power radio link to a gateway on the wall. The gateway forwards the readings to the service, which stores them for two years and compares each one with the room's limits. When a reading is out of range for longer than the delay you chose, the person on the rota gets a text message and a phone call, and the alert stays open until someone records what was done about it.

What does a site need?

  • One sensor for each room, freezer or van.
  • One gateway for every forty sensors within the same building.
  • A power socket and a network cable or a mobile signal for the gateway.
