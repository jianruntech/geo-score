Frostline pricing

Frostline is sold per sensor, with the gateway included for every forty sensors. Each plan includes the alerts by text message and phone call, two years of stored readings, the inspector's report and support by phone during working hours. There is no charge for adding users to the rota.

What does a plan include?

  • Starter: up to five sensors, one site, alerts to three people.
  • Business: up to forty sensors per site, any number of sites, alerts to any number of people.
  • Enterprise: sensors in vans as well as rooms, single sign-on and an agreement on support response times.

How is a site billed?

Each site is billed monthly or yearly, from the day its first sensor reports. Sensors that are removed stop being billed at the end of the month, and a replaced sensor keeps the history of the one it replaces, so the record an inspector reads has no break in it. Ask for a quote for more than two hundred sensors.
