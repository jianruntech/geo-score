# CLI versus hand audit

Generated by `benchmark/validity.py` on 2026-10-02 with geo-score-cli/1.5.0 (rubric v1.1). Do not edit by hand; re-run the script.
中文版：[VALIDITY.zh-CN.md](VALIDITY.zh-CN.md)

## What was measured

Each of the 5 published hand audits in [examples/audits/v1.1](../examples/audits/v1.1/) was re-scored by the CLI on the same pages (`--urls` pinned to the audit's `sampled_urls`), and every check both of them scored was compared tier for tier: 84 check pairs.

This compares the CLI with a human reading of the same rubric. Whether the readiness score goes with being cited is a separate question, set out before any data in the [preregistered validation plan](PREREGISTRATION.md).

## Result

- **On the checks both of them scored, the CLI read 2.4 points lower on average.** Per site, the CLI's share of those checks' points minus the auditor's ranged from 12 lower to 10 higher (mean absolute difference 8.8). Bland-Altman (n=5): bias -2.4, 95% limits of agreement -21.7 to +16.9.
- **On the full normalised score the CLI read 5.2 points lower on average**, ranging from 16 lower to 7 higher. The two figures differ because the auditor also scored p3.listings, p3.mentions and p4.question-coverage, which the CLI leaves out of its denominator.
- **Same tier on 80% of check pairs.** Checks read by rule agreed on 92% (39 pairs); checks the CLI approximates with a heuristic agreed on 69% (45 pairs). The 3 judgement checks are never scored by the CLI.

## Read this before quoting it

- N=5 sites, each hand-scored once by one auditor; this is a first measurement, not a validation study.
- The hand audits are dated; the CLI re-scored the live pages later, so site changes are inside every difference.
- The CLI samples only the audited host; hand-sampled pages on other hosts were dropped.
- The three judgement checks (p3.listings, p3.mentions, p4.question-coverage) are never scored by the CLI and are not compared; p4.cn-engines is compared only where both the CLI and the auditor scored it.
- The 5 sites are the ones whose v1.0 audits calibrated v1.1 ([calibration record](../rubric/calibration-v1.1.md)), so this is development data, not a held-out set.
- The hand scores run from 69 to 87; 1 of the 5 sites is Chinese-language and 0 are gate-capped, so low-scoring and capped sites are not represented.
- The audit files do not record who scored them, or whether the scorer had seen the CLI's output.
