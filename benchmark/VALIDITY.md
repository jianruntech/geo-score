## What was measured

Each of the 5 published hand audits in [examples/audits/v1.1](../examples/audits/v1.1/) was re-scored by the CLI on the same pages (`--urls` pinned to the audit's `sampled_urls`), and every check both of them scored was compared tier for tier: 86 check pairs.

## Result

- **Same tier on 78% of check pairs.** Checks read by rule agreed on 95% (41 pairs); checks the CLI approximates with a heuristic agreed on 62% (45 pairs). The 3 judgement checks are never scored by the CLI.
- **The CLI's normalised score was 4.2 points lower on average**, ranging from 13 lower to 6 higher.

## Read this before quoting it

- N=5 sites, each hand-scored once by one auditor; this is a first measurement, not a validation study.
- The hand audits are dated; the CLI re-scored the live pages later, so site changes are inside every difference.
- The CLI samples only the audited host; hand-sampled pages on other hosts were dropped.
- The three judgement checks (p3.listings, p3.mentions, p4.question-coverage) are never scored by the CLI and are not compared; p4.cn-engines is compared only where both the CLI and the auditor scored it.
