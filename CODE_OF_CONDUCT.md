# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be direct about the work and decent to the people. Technical disagreement is welcome and
expected — especially about the rubric, which is the whole point of this repository.
Personal attacks, harassment, and bad-faith argument are not.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is
representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report to **hello@jianruntech.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers and handled
privately. Depending on severity, responses range from a request to edit a comment
through to a ban.

Maintainers who do not enforce this in good faith may be removed from the project.
