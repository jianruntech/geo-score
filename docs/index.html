A quarter of the field is unreadable to AI crawlers

73 sites have a gate check at zero — a retrieval crawler cannot get the content, so nothing else on the page matters. Three very different problems live inside that number.

DELIBERATE — 20

Their robots.txt disallows AI retrieval crawlers by name. A site that does not want to be quoted is not misconfigured, and this rubric reports it as it is.

aftership.com, amazon.com, arstechnica.com, drugs.com, ebay.com, elegoo.com, figma.com, healthline.com, jdcloud.com, june.so, kuehne-nagel.com, lexisnexis.com, mercadolibre.com, notion.com, phoronix.com, qcloud.com, techcrunch.com, theregister.com, theverge.com, wired.com

DELIBERATE OR ACCIDENTAL — 12

The server refuses crawler user-agents. The probes carry the crawlers' user-agent strings but come from the auditor's own address, unverified, so a server that checks a crawler by its IP address may refuse them and still serve the real crawler. From outside, a policy and a bot-protection default look the same.

airbnb.com, bambulab.com, clubfactory.com, cma-cgm.com, deepseek.com, hashicorp.com, hims.com, lenovo.com, lwn.net, marriott.com, shopline.com, stratechery.com

ACCIDENTAL — 41

Body copy only exists after JavaScript runs. The content is there and a browser sees it; a retrieval crawler gets an empty shell. This group almost certainly did not choose it. 30 of them were scored on fewer than 4 pages.

Check your own site

No install, no dependencies, no account: Python 3.8+ is all it needs.

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jianruntech/geo-score/v1.8.0/cli/geo_score.py \
  | python3 - yoursite.com

Add --explain for the evidence behind every check and what the next tier asks for, or --compare competitor.com to see the two side by side.

We ran the whole benchmark twice on 2026-10-12, with geo-score-cli/1.8.0, and compared every site: 99% of sites land within ±5 and 81% land identically, so read one site's number as ±5 rather than as exact. Medians are stable. The gate-cap state flipped on 1 sites between the two runs. This measures readiness, not outcomes: whether an engine can cite a site. Whether one does depends on competition and query intent, which no site-side audit can observe. Three checks that need off-site search or human judgement are left out of the denominator rather than guessed, and a fourth, Chinese engine readiness, unless the site serves Chinese, so these run lower than a hand-scored audit. Sites change; every number carries the date it was measured.
